Privacy Policy

Effective 4 October 2026

In short: Sameish has no accounts and never asks for your email, phone number or contacts. Your progress stays on your device. We use a few services to measure how the game is played, fix crashes, show ads and handle purchases, and they receive limited technical data described below.

Data that stays on your device

Your puzzle results, streak, achievements, settings and the name you choose are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. Your name leaves the device only if you share a challenge link or progress picture yourself, and then only with the people you send it to.

Data shared with service providers

ServiceWhat it receivesWhy
PostHog (analytics)Gameplay events such as "puzzle completed", time taken, difficulty and whether a share or purchase happened; an anonymous random ID; device model, OS and app version; approximate location derived from IP address.Understand how the game is played and improve it. Not used for advertising.
Sentry (crash reports)Error details, device model, OS and app version when the app crashes.Find and fix bugs.
Google AdMob (ads)Device and advertising identifiers, IP address and ad interaction data, as needed to serve ads, prevent fraud and limit how often an ad is shown.Show ads. We request non-personalised ads only, and Sameish does not track you across other apps.
RevenueCat, Apple and Google (purchases)An anonymous app user ID and your purchase history in Sameish. Payment details are handled by Apple or Google and never reach us.Unlock and restore purchases.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it to build advertising profiles.

Notifications

If you turn on daily reminders, they are scheduled on your device. No notification data is sent to a server.

Children

Sameish is a general-audience puzzle game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and ads are requested as non-personalised and limited to general-audience content.

Your choices

  • You can play without entering a name.
  • Reminders are off until you turn them on.
  • On iOS you can reset your advertising identifier in Settings > Privacy; on Android in Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. To ask about or delete data held by our service providers, email us with the subject "Sameish data request".

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
